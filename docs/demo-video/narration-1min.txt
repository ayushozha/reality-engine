Hi I am Ayush and I made this with Kush.

A phone video shows a room, but it doesn't give you objects you can move or use in a simulator.

We built Reality Engine to turn that walkthrough into an editable 3D starting point.

OpenCV recovers camera positions and a point cloud. Gemini 4 Argon reads the whole video in one context, places objects, then compares renders against the original footage and repairs the scene.

Here's our actual phone clip and its reconstruction: sixteen editable objects after two critique rounds.

In the editor, I can select and move objects, compare against the original photo, and export to glTF or MuJoCo.

It's a coarse prototype, but the complete video-to-editable-scene loop works.
