We built Reality Engine because a video shows you a space, but it doesn't give you objects you can move, measure, or use in a simulator.

Our goal is to turn a phone walkthrough into an editable 3D starting point.

Here's how it works. OpenCV selects stable frames, reconstructs the camera path and a colored point cloud, and estimates the floor.

Gemini 4 Argon reads the whole video in one context. Zone workers inherit that reading and place named objects against the measured cameras and points. The engine supports running those workers in parallel.

Then we render the reconstruction from the original camera positions. Argon watches real-versus-replica comparisons and revises the objects. That's our generate, render, critique, and repair loop for the More Dimensions track.
